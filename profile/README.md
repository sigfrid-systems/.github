Sigfrid Systems is a Swedish technology company working in software and AI. The systems we build are meant to be understood by the people who depend on them, and to keep working as their needs change. Most of our work happens in private repositories, so what a visitor sees here says more about how we think than about what we ship.

## What we believe

The quality of a system is settled before anyone sees it: in the data model, in the boundaries drawn between parts, and in what each part promises the others. We spend our effort there. A system rarely becomes better than the foundations it was given, so the foundations are where a decision deserves the most care.

Almost every system stands or falls on its data. Interfaces get rewritten and services are replaced, while the data lives on, so we design data to keep its meaning and fidelity as it moves between systems and over the years.

Regulation belongs in the craft. Data protection, accountability, and the rules of each sector are inputs to the architecture, and a system that carries them from the start earns trust by how it works rather than by what is promised about it. The same holds for the agreements between parts of a system: each one states what it accepts, what it returns, and who may call it, in a form that can be checked.

A check that was never run is not a pass, so we prefer gates that fail loudly over gates that look green. When we use AI, it assists (it guides, drafts, searches, and explains) while judgement and responsibility stay with the people accountable for the outcome.

Engagements begin with a bounded problem, a stated way of testing the result, and a first step that can be finished on its own. Every claim we make about our work should be literally true.

## How we work

We are small and founder-led; every engagement is a reference, and our reputation is the company. We work remotely, write things down, and keep decisions and their reasons where they can be reviewed later.
